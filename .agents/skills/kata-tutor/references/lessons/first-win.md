# First win

**Practice:** State one useful outcome and define done for a small real task.

Concepts: [outcome](../concepts/outcome.md) and [done](../concepts/done.md).

1. Ask what should be different when the work succeeds and why it matters.
2. Help replace an activity with an observable result.
3. Ask for one completion check the result consumer can inspect.
4. Give focused feedback and let the learner revise.

Worked example:

```text
Activity: Research our options.
Outcome: A decision brief compares three available options for the team lead.
Done when: Each option has a source, cost range, main constraint, and a stated
recommendation; unsupported facts are marked unknown.
```

Complete when the learner produces an outcome with an observable completion
check. Label a copied or heavily guided answer as demonstrated or guided.
